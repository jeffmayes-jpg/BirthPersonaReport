Birth Persona Report — Numerology, Biorhythm Graph, and Person-Specific Astrology Update
Visual Studio 2022 / VB.NET / WinForms / .NET 10.
This version keeps the working tabbed report structure and adds:
    • Numerology: Life Path Number, Soul Urge Number, and Birthday Number.
    • Soul Urge calculation from both the full birth name and, when supplied, the nickname.
    • Explicit explanation that Life Path and Birthday Numbers come from the date of birth and therefore do not change with a nickname.
    • A graphical biorhythm display showing the physical, emotional, and intellectual cycles around today's date.
    • Person-specific Western astrology particulars: Sun Sign, element, modality, and ruling planet, followed by an interpretation for the individual.
    • Person-specific Chinese astrology particulars: animal, element, and Yin/Yang polarity, followed by an interpretation for the individual.
    • The complete traditional Monday's Child poem first in the Day of Birth chapter, followed by an explanation and then the person's date and corresponding line.
The report continues to present numerology, biorhythm, astrology, and Chinese astrology as traditional/interpretive systems rather than scientifically established predictors.
PDF export contains the complete textual report. The biorhythm graph is displayed in the application; the PDF chapter describes the graph and its numerical values.
Famous People selection: click any row to select the entire person, then click Use Selected, or double-click the row. The selected person's name, birth date, and nickname are returned to the main screen.
