Birth Persona Report

Numerology, Biorhythm Graph, and Person-Specific Astrology

Birth Persona Report is a Windows desktop application built with VB.NET, WinForms, and .NET 10. It brings several traditional and interpretive systems together in a tabbed, person-specific report.

Download BirthPersonaReport (ZIP) Project details on GitHub

Download the ZIP, extract its contents, and run the application executable from the extracted folder.

Features

  • Numerology: Life Path Number, Soul Urge Number, and Birthday Number.
  • Soul Urge: Calculated from the full birth name and, when supplied, the nickname.
  • Birth date calculations: Explains why Life Path and Birthday Numbers do not change with a nickname.
  • Biorhythm graph: Displays physical, emotional, and intellectual cycles around today's date.
  • Western astrology: Sun Sign, element, modality, and ruling planet, followed by an interpretation for the individual.
  • Chinese astrology: Animal, element, and Yin/Yang polarity, followed by an interpretation for the individual.
  • Monday's Child: Includes the complete traditional poem, an explanation, and the person's date and corresponding line.

PDF Export

PDF export contains the complete textual report. The biorhythm graph is displayed in the application; the PDF chapter describes the graph and its numerical values.

Famous People Selection

Click a row to select a person, then click Use Selected, or double-click the row. The selected person's name, birth date, and nickname are returned to the main screen.

Important Note

Numerology, biorhythm, Western astrology, and Chinese astrology are presented as traditional or interpretive systems rather than scientifically established predictors.
